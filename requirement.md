## **1. Design & Brand Identity**

* **Theme Reference:** [bcdapps.io](http://bcdapps.io)
* **Visual Style:**
* **Vibe:** Corporate, Trustworthy, High-Tech, Minimalist.
* **Backgrounds:** Clean white (`#FFFFFF`) or off-white (`#F9F9F9`) for content; Deep Navy Blue (`#0F172A`) for Hero sections and Footer to match the "Enterprise" feel.
